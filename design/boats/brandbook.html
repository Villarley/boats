The waterline

Wherever money is drawn, the crew is blue and sits above a navy line. The captain's stake is red and sits below it, the color of a hull under water. Losses come off the bottom first.

Shape and space

Radius, borders and where things sit.

Corners nest. Edges align. Actions sit under the thumb.

8Score chips, tags
10Icon tiles
16Cards, buttons, inputs
28Bottom sheets
40Screen corner
PillFilter chips only
Concentric cornersA shape inside another takes the outer radius minus the gap between them. Nothing looks pinched at the corners.
Border or shadow, never bothCards on paper get a 1px #E3E6EB border. Only floating things (sheets, toasts) get a shadow.
Pills mean "choose"Fully round shapes are only for filter chips. Buttons and cards share radius 16.
8pt spacing4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32. Screen gutter 20. Card padding 16. Gap between cards 12.
One left edgeTitles, card content and list text share the 20px gutter. Dividers start where the text starts, not at the icon.
Thumb zonePrimary actions sit at the bottom of the screen, full width, 52px tall, 8px apart. Nothing that moves money sits at the top.
Numbers align rightAmounts and percentages use tabular figures and line up on the right edge of rows.
Icons in tiles18px icon centered in a 36px tile with radius 10. Rows are at least 56px tall.
No separators in textMetadata is split into icon and label pairs, never joined with dots or pipes.

Flows

Steps, then what can go wrong.

Seven flows, edge cases included.

Mom joinsFamily

  1. Ana sends a link on WhatsApp
  2. Mom sees the boat and its worst case
  3. Face ID
  4. Adds dollars
  5. Boards
No Face ID: device code, never a password.Link expired (48 h): one tap to ask Ana for a new one.Opened on a laptop: QR code to continue on the phone.

Board a boatCrew

  1. Explore
  2. Boat page
  3. Amount
  4. Review: worst case and lock
  5. Face ID
  6. Receipt
Not enough dollars: add them without leaving the flow.Boarding closed: "Tell me when the next voyage opens".Failure: "Your money hasn't moved. Try again."

While it sailsCrew

  1. Push: captain traded
  2. Boat view
  3. Push: the contract said no
  4. Open on arcscan
Wants out mid-voyage: show the lock date and the floor, offer an alert.Near the floor: alert at −7%, editable.

Back in portCrew

  1. Floor, goal or date
  2. checkVoyage()
  3. Market vs you
  4. Withdraw or board again
Slippage: show the real figure, "$988, $2 lost selling".Goal reached: same screen, "The boat made 15%. You made 12%."

Launch a boatCaptain

  1. Name and flag
  2. Assets and max weights
  3. Floor, goal, length
  4. Stake and fee
  5. Review with score
  6. Face ID deposits stake
  7. Share link
Stake under 10%: allowed, the score drops and says why.Floor past −25%: blocked, with the reason.

TradeCaptain

  1. Pick from and to
  2. Live rule check
  3. Face ID
  4. Contract executes or reverts
  5. Crew is notified
Breaks a rule: names the rule and offers the largest valid amount.Sent anyway by API: the contract reverts it, and the attempt is public.

AI captainCaptain

  1. Choose AI captain
  2. Rules in plain words or by voice
  3. Pick the Gemini market
  4. Every decision logged
Wants to leave the rules: it can't, it only calls swap().Market data down: holds position and logs why.
